🚀 Important: How to publish your changes

When you click "Save Changes", the content is saved to your browser so you can preview it immediately on your computer. However, because your site is hosted statically, other people will not see the updates yet!

To make changes live for everyone:
1. Click "Save Changes".
2. Click "Download data.json".
3. Upload and replace the data.json file in your GitHub/hosting provider.
